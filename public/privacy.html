Nubble Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nubble collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it simple because our approach to your data is simple.

Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Nubble:

  • If you sign in with Google: we receive your email address and name from Google to create and identify your account.
  • Your notes: the notes, bubbles, tags, and organization you create are stored securely in our database provider, Supabase, so they can sync across your devices.

Guest Mode

If you use Nubble in guest mode, we collect no personal information. Your notes are stored only locally on your device and are never sent to our servers.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide and operate Nubble — storing your notes, syncing them across your devices, and identifying your account. That's it.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or any other purpose. Your data is only handled by the infrastructure services required to run the app (such as Supabase for storage and Google for sign-in).

Analytics & Tracking

Nubble contains no analytics and no tracking. We do not use cookies for tracking, advertising identifiers, or any third-party analytics tools.

Deleting Your Account & Data

You are in control of your data. You can delete your notes at any time from within the app, and you may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below. Once deleted, your data is removed from our database.

Contact
