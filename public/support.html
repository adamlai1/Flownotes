Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a note?

Tap the + button to create a note. It's added to whichever bubble you currently have open, so open a bubble first if you want the note filed there. Start typing, add tags if you like, and your changes save automatically — there's no save button to hunt for.

How do bubbles work?

Bubbles are how Nubble organizes your thinking. Each bubble is a cluster of related notes, and bubbles can be nested inside other bubbles, so a broad topic can branch into narrower ones as your thoughts develop.

Tap a bubble to dive into it and see what's inside. Hold and drag to rearrange bubbles. A note can belong to more than one bubble, and you can also connect notes to each other to show how ideas relate.

How do I sign in and sync my notes?

Sign in with Google from the sign-in screen. Once you're signed in, your notes, bubbles, and tags sync automatically across every device you use with that account, including changes you made while offline.

You can also continue as a guest. Guest notes stay on your device only and are never uploaded — which also means they don't sync and aren't recoverable if you clear your browser data. Signing in later moves you onto the synced account.

How do I delete a note?

Open the note and tap the trash icon, or use the Delete option on the note card itself. You'll be asked to confirm, since deleting a note is permanent and can't be undone. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email us at the address above.
